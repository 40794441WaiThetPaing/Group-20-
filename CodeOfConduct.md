# Team Code of Conduct
## 1. Professionalism and Respect
Treat all team members, stakeholders, and users with respect, courtesy, and fairness at all times.

Act with integrity and honesty in all interactions. Create an open and supportive team environment.

## Collaboration and Communication
Communicate effectively: share ideas, feedback, and concerns openly and constructively.

Support team decisions and respect consensus, even if you personally disagree.

Respond promptly to team communications and requests, honoring deadlines and agreed meeting times.

If you're unable to meet a commitment, notify the team as soon as possible.

## 3. Contribution and Responsibility
Contribute actively and equitably to team deliverables, sprints, and code reviews.

Take ownership for your tasks; deliver work to the best of your ability, and seek help when needed.

Do not claim or take credit for the work of others.

## 4. Integrity and Honesty
Do not plagiarize or submit work which is not your own.

## 5. Commitment to Quality
Strive for high standards in code quality, documentation, testing, and version control.

Follow all Git, Docker, and CI/CD protocols agreed upon by the team.

Ensure your work is reviewed and tested before merging.

## 6. Upholding the Mission
Keep the goals of the coursework and benefits to the organization as your main professional priority.

If you disagree with an approach, propose improvements constructively, rather than obstructing progress.

## 7. Conflict Resolution
Address disagreements calmly, professionally, and privately in the first instance.

If conflicts cannot be resolved within the team, escalate to the teaching team as per module guidelines.

## 8. Compliance

Breaches of this Code may result in removal from the team and failure of the coursework, according to university and module policy.
